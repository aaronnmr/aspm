RICE Scoring

Formula: Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort

Example: Reach = 200, Impact = 4, Confidence = 80, Effort = 5 → Score = (200 × 4 × 80) / 5 = 12,800

Use Confidence as a percentage (0–100). Use Reach, Impact and Effort as numeric values.
